Eventual Consistency

Cassandra lets you pick the consistency level per operation. Higher levels (QUORUM, ALL) give stronger guarantees but more latency; lower levels (ONE) are faster but may read stale data until replicas converge.

Levels at a glance

LevelReads / writes acknowledgedTradeoff
ONE1 replicaFastest, may be stale
QUORUMmajorityBalanced (recommended)
ALLevery replicaStrongest, slowest
