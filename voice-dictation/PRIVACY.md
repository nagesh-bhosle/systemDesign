# Voice Dictation — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Summary

Voice Dictation is designed to keep your voice and transcripts on your Mac. We do not run analytics or sell data.

## Audio

Speech recognition runs on your Mac by default (Apple on-device recognition). If on-device recognition is unavailable, the app may use Apple's server-based speech recognition. Audio is not sent to Voice Dictation's developers.

## Optional text cleanup (LLM)

If you enable LLM cleanup and provide an API key, **transcribed text only** (not audio) is sent to Abacus AI for grammar and punctuation cleanup. You can disable this at any time.

## History

Transcript history is stored locally in your Application Support folder. It is encrypted on disk and excluded from Time Machine backups when possible. You can disable history saving or clear history in Settings.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — to record your voice for transcription.
- **Speech Recognition** — to convert speech to text.
- **Accessibility** — optional; used to paste at your cursor and detect secure fields. After rebuilding the app, you may need to uncheck and recheck Voice Dictation in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

## Contact

This is a local macOS utility. No account is required.
