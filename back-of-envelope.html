10 60-second worked example

"Chat app: 100M DAU, each sends 2 messages/day, 1 KB per message, 20 reads per write."

EstimateResult
Write QPS2×108 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 2.3K avg → ~5K peak
Read QPS20 × 2.3K ≈ 46K avg → ~93K peak
Storage205 GB/day → 75 TB/yr → ~375 TB (5 yr) → ~1.1 PB with RF=3
Egress46K × 1 KB × 8 ≈ 0.38 Gbps
Discord connection: at 200K messages/day/channel, a 100K-row partition cap holds about half a day of traffic — Discord's 10-day bucket works because their per-partition budget is much larger. The same formula bucket = total budget ÷ write rate drives the choice.
Interviewers never check exact digits — they check whether your orders of magnitude are sane and whether you state assumptions out loud before computing.
